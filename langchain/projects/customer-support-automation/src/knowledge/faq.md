# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What products does ShopEase sell?

ShopEase sells:

- Laptops
- Mechanical Keyboards
- Gaming Keyboards
- Wireless Mouse
- Gaming Mouse
- Monitors
- Headphones
- Smartphone Accessories

---

## How can I track my order?

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive an email containing a tracking number.

You can also ask our support assistant for your order status.

---

## How can I contact customer support?

Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Support requests can also be submitted through our AI assistant.

---

## Can I update my shipping address?

Customers may update their shipping address before the order has been shipped.

After shipment, the shipping address cannot be changed.

---

## What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept:

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay

---

## Do you ship internationally?

Currently ShopEase only ships within the United States.
