# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery.

Products must:

- Be unused
- Be in original packaging
- Include all accessories

Refunds are issued using the original payment method.

Refund processing usually takes between 3 and 5 business days after inspection.

The following products are not eligible for refunds:

- Gift cards
- Downloadable software
- Customized products

Damaged or defective products qualify for a full refund or replacement.
