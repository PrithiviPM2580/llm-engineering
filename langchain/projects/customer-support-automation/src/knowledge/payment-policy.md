# Payment Policy

Accepted payment methods:

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay

Payments are securely processed using encrypted payment gateways.

ShopEase never stores customer credit card information.

If a payment fails:

- Verify card information.
- Check available balance.
- Contact your bank.
- Try another payment method.

Orders are confirmed only after successful payment.
