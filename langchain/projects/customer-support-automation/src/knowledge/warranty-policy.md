# Warranty Policy

All electronic products include a manufacturer's warranty.

Warranty periods:

Laptops

24 months

Monitors

24 months

Mechanical Keyboards

12 months

Gaming Mouse

12 months

Headphones

12 months

Warranty covers:

- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failure under normal use

Warranty does not cover:

- Physical damage
- Liquid damage
- Unauthorized repairs
- Accidental misuse

Customers should keep their purchase receipt for warranty claims.
