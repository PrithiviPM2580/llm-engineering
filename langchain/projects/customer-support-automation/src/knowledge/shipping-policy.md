# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time

Orders are processed within 24 hours after successful payment.

Orders placed during weekends or holidays are processed on the next business day.

---

## Shipping Methods

Standard Shipping

Estimated delivery:

3–5 business days.

Express Shipping

Estimated delivery:

1–2 business days.

---

## Order Tracking

Tracking information is emailed immediately after an order ships.

Customers can also check shipment status through customer support.

---

## Shipping Delays

Unexpected weather conditions, carrier delays, or public holidays may increase delivery times.

---

## Lost Packages

If an order has not arrived within 10 business days after shipment, customers should contact customer support.
